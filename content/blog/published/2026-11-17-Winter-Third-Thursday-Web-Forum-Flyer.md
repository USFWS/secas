The Third Thursday Web Forum schedule for the next three months is now available! We'll skip the December webinar for the holidays, but we have great speakers lined up for January and February. SECAS hosts these webinars on the third Thursday of each month at 10 am Eastern time. [»Click here for an interactive pdf of the flyer](/pdf/ThirdThursdayWebForumFlyer_Winter2026_sm.pdf), with functioning links.

<!--more-->

- **December 17th @ 10 am ET:** _No webinar this month--enjoy the holidays!_
- **January 21st @ 10 am ET:** _Developing Southeast region-wide aquatic Conservation Opportunity Areas (COAs) using habitat suitability modeling of Regional Species of Greatest Conservation Need (RSGCN)._ This month's presentation by Todd Ewing, Program Manager for the Southeast Aquatic Resources Partnership, will talk about a project to develop region-wide COAs for the aquatic species in the Southeast using habitat suitability models for aquatic RSGCN. Register here: [https://events.gcc.teams.microsoft.com/event/152454b7-27e4-47fb-95b9-a6644878e547@0693b5ba-4b18-4d7b-9341-f32f400a5494](https://events.gcc.teams.microsoft.com/event/152454b7-27e4-47fb-95b9-a6644878e547@0693b5ba-4b18-4d7b-9341-f32f400a5494).
- **February 18th @ 10 am ET:** _The Florida model: Landscape-scale conservation science, planning, and design._ Tom Hoctor, Director of the Center for Landscape Conservation Planning at the University of Florida, will discuss the history, science, and current status of regional landscape scale conservation efforts in Florida including the Florida Ecological Greenways Network (FEGN) and the Florida Wildlife Corridor (FWC). Register here: [https://events.gcc.teams.microsoft.com/event/4b849227-a291-4a6f-bee8-5e4cc2ad0cc6@0693b5ba-4b18-4d7b-9341-f32f400a5494](https://events.gcc.teams.microsoft.com/event/4b849227-a291-4a6f-bee8-5e4cc2ad0cc6@0693b5ba-4b18-4d7b-9341-f32f400a5494).
