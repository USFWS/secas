The [Caribbean Conservation Community of Practice](https://secasc.ncsu.edu/resources-retired2026/caribbean-community-of-practice/) (CCCoP) is composed of community leaders, researchers, universities, non-profits, as well as federal, state, and territorial governments. All partners are working together to build an applied science and resource management network.

The CCCoP holds an annual in-person meeting that alternates between the U.S. Virgin Islands and Puerto Rico. Please join us November 17-19 in Bayamón, Puerto Rico to hear from local researchers and practitioners and engage in discussions around emerging conservation needs in the U.S. Caribbean.

<!--more-->

This year, the CCCoP will be co-hosting the meeting with the Coastal Zone Management Symposium, organized by PR DRNA, providing a unique opportunity to connect with partners on broader topics. The final day of the meeting will be dedicated to field trips, where attendees can see first-hand the incredible work happening across Puerto Rico.

This is a great time to connect with conservation partners in the U.S. Caribbean!

**[If you're interested in attending, sign up for the CCCoP listserv](https://groups.google.com/g/caribbean-conservation-community-of-practice/about)**. More details about the agenda and hotel accommodations will be shared through the listserv.

We welcome you to share this with your networks. If you have any questions, you can reach out to me directly at [alexandria_lamle@fws.gov](mailto:Alexandria_lamle@fws.gov). Additionally, you can find agendas from past meetings on the [CCCoP website](https://secasc.ncsu.edu/resources-retired2026/caribbean-community-of-practice/). 

We hope to see you in Bayamón this November!
